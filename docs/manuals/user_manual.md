# DentalCare Pro - Clinical & Practice Operations User Manual

## Welcome to DentalCare Pro
DentalCare Pro is a unified, enterprise-grade Dental Practice Management System. This manual provides role-based operating instructions for daily practice tasks.

---

## 1. Clinician Workflow (Dentists & Dental Specialists)

### 1.1 Managing the Daily Operatory Schedule
1. Log in to the web portal or open the **DentalCare Pro Mobile App**.
2. Navigate to **Appointments / Chairside Schedule**.
3. View the chairside timeline:
   - Yellow badge: `CHECKED_IN` (Patient in waiting room).
   - Blue badge: `IN_TREATMENT` (Patient seated in operatory chair).
   - Green badge: `COMPLETED` (Clinical procedures finished).
4. Click on an appointment to open the **Patient Clinical Chart**.

### 1.2 Using the Interactive 32-Tooth Odontogram
1. Select the **Odontogram** tab.
2. Select numbering mode: **FDI** (default) or **Universal**.
3. Click on any tooth (e.g. Tooth #14):
   - Choose anatomical surface: Mesial, Distal, Occlusal, Buccal, Lingual.
   - Select condition: **Caries (Red)**, **Composite Filling (Blue)**, **Crown (Yellow)**, **Root Canal (Purple)**, **Missing (Grey)**.
4. Click **Record Finding**. The surface instantly highlights with international color standards.

### 1.3 Recording SOAP Clinical Notes
1. Open the **SOAP Notes** drawer.
2. Complete the 4 standard clinical sections:
   - **Subjective (S)**: Patient's chief complaint and symptoms in their own words.
   - **Objective (O)**: Clinical observations, periodontal probing depths, radiograph findings.
   - **Assessment (A)**: Diagnostic conclusion (e.g., reversible pulpitis, chronic periodontitis).
   - **Plan (P)**: Recommended treatment procedures, restorative materials, follow-up intervals.
3. Use the **AI Dictate / Summarize** button to convert spoken voice notes into formatted bullet points.
4. Click **Sign & Seal Clinical Note**.

### 1.4 Generating E-Prescriptions
1. Navigate to **Prescriptions -> New Prescription**.
2. Search generic or brand medicine names (e.g. Amoxicillin, Ibuprofen).
3. Specify dosage, frequency (OD, BD, TDS, QID), duration, and food timing.
4. The system automatically cross-references patient allergy records.
5. Click **Issue & Transmit**. The digital prescription is instantly accessible on the Patient Mobile App and available as a signed PDF.

## 2. Front Desk & Receptionist Workflow

### 2.1 Patient Registration & Medical Intake
1. Navigate to **Patients -> New Patient Registration**.
2. Capture demographic details: Full Name, Date of Birth, Gender, Mobile Number, Government ID (Aadhaar / National ID).
3. Complete the digital **Medical History Questionnaire** (allergies, diabetes, hypertension, cardiac conditions, medications).
4. Have the patient sign the digital **Informed Consent Form** on the front-desk tablet.

### 2.2 QR-Code Check-In & Queue Management
1. arrivers present their digital QR pass from the Patient Mobile App.
2. Open the camera scanner on the receptionist terminal or mobile app.
3. Scanning the QR code automatically marks the patient as `CHECKED_IN` and alerts the assigned dental hygienist.

### 2.3 Point-of-Sale (POS) Billing & Invoicing
1. Once the dentist marks treatment complete, open **Billing -> Generate Invoice**.
2. Review automatically imported procedures (CDT / ADA dental procedure codes).
3. Apply clinic discounts or insurance co-pays as applicable.
4. Select payment method: **Credit/Debit Card**, **Cash**, **UPI QR Code**, or **Insurance Pre-Auth**.
5. Issue the receipt via SMS, Email, or printable thermal slip.

## 3. Patient Portal & Mobile Experience

1. **Self-Service Scheduling**: Patients can select preferred clinicians, treatment categories, and available time slots.
2. **Medical Vault**: 24/7 access to diagnostic X-rays, treatment history, and active medication instructions.
3. **Contactless Payments**: Review itemized statements and settle copay balances online securely.
