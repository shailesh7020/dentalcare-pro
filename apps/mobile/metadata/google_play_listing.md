### Full Description (4000 characters max):
**DentalCare Pro** is the enterprise-grade, cloud-native dental practice management platform engineered for modern dental clinics, multi-branch healthcare organizations, and hospital dental departments.

Whether you are a solo practitioner, an associate dentist, a practice manager, or a patient, DentalCare Pro unifies your entire clinical and administrative workflow into a cohesive, secure, cross-platform mobile experience.

#### KEY CLINICAL FEATURES (FOR DENTISTS & HYGIENISTS):
- **Interactive 32-Tooth Odontogram**: Rapid FDI and Universal numbering charting with visual condition tagging (Caries, Crowns, Root Canals, Restorations, Extractions).
- **Intraoral Camera Studio**: Capture high-resolution clinical photographs, tag individual tooth surfaces, and automatically compress images by up to 84% for instant cloud sync.
- **Structured SOAP Notes**: Fast chairside clinical documentation with voice transcription and AI-assisted summaries.
- **Treatment Plan Builder**: Estimate procedures, map tooth numbers, and capture digital patient consent signatures directly on your device.
- **Chairside Digital Prescriptions**: Formulate compliant prescriptions with built-in contraindication reminders.

#### FRONT-DESK & PRACTICE MANAGEMENT (FOR RECEPTIONISTS):
- **Contactless QR Check-In**: Scan arriving patients for instantaneous arrival logging and operatory queue assignment.
- **Live Waiting Queue**: Real-time operatory chair status and waiting room oversight.
- **Point-of-Sale Mobile Billing**: Issue itemized invoices, record cash, card, and UPI payments, and generate digital receipts.
- **Real-Time Calendar**: Multi-chair, multi-provider daily and weekly scheduling.

#### PATIENT PORTAL & HEALTH HUB (FOR PATIENTS):
- **Appointment Booking & Check-In**: Self-service slot reservation with instant confirmation and digital passes.
- **Prescription & Invoice Vault**: View medication instructions, dosage timings, and itemized billing statements.
- **Secure Messaging**: HIPAA-compliant two-way communication with your clinical care team.

#### ENTERPRISE SECURITY & RELIABILITY:
- **Offline-First Synchronization**: Work seamlessly during internet interruptions; all clinical records synchronize automatically once connectivity is restored.
- **Biometric Hardware Protection**: Face ID and Fingerprint hardware authentication protecting Protected Health Information (PHI).
- **HIPAA & GDPR Ready**: AES-256 data encryption at rest and in transit.
